One-line: Circular translucent control for carousels, media playback and toolbar affordances.

```jsx
<IconButton icon="play" label="Play" tone="on-dark" />
```

- Fill is `rgba(0,0,0,0.05)`; the glyph is white over dark surfaces, ink over light ones.
- Takes the same `scale(0.9)` press transform as the marketing pills.
